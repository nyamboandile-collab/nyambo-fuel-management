🔐 Reset Business Data

This removes transactional records so you can start a new accounting period or clean test data.

This will delete: pump readings, credit sales, payments, payment alerts, cash sales, fuel purchases, stock records, expenses and delivery/movement records.

It will NOT delete: your garages, pumps, fuel tanks, customers, suppliers or fuel products.
